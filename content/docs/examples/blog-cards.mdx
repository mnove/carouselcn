## Use Case

Blog card carousels are perfect for:
- Blog post previews on landing pages
- News and article sections
- Content marketing showcases
- Related posts suggestions

## Features

- **Multi-column layout** - Shows 3 articles at once
- **Category badges** - Visual tags for content classification
- **Rich metadata** - Author, date, and reading time
- **Excerpt preview** - Text truncation with line-clamp
- **Hover effects** - Image zoom on interaction

## Tips

- Use `line-clamp-2` or `line-clamp-3` to truncate long text consistently
- Keep excerpts concise (2-3 sentences) for better scannability
- Consider lazy loading images for performance with large content libraries
- Use consistent aspect ratios (`aspect-video`) for uniform appearance
