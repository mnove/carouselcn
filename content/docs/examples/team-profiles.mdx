## Use Case

Team profile carousels are ideal for:
- Company team pages and about sections
- Leadership bios and executive showcases
- Staff directories and department overviews
- Conference speaker lineups

## Features

- **Circular avatars** - Professional headshot display
- **Role hierarchy** - Clear job titles and positions
- **Bio snippets** - Brief descriptions of expertise
- **Social links** - LinkedIn and Twitter integration
- **Centered layout** - Clean, professional presentation

## Tips

- Use consistent image dimensions (square crops) for uniform avatars
- Keep bios to 1-2 sentences for scannability
- Consider adding team member initials as fallback for missing photos
- Use semantic HTML with proper ARIA labels for social links
